Getting help

Found a bug, or have a question or an idea? Open a support request and describe what happened, on which device. Requests are posted publicly, so please do not include personal information.

1.There is no sound

Check that your iPhone or iPad is not in silent mode and that the volume is up, and that the title screen shows SOUND ON. Sound starts after your first tap.

2.What counts as a cut

Close the jaws while a painted link that matches the cut order sits between them; white brackets mark the link in the jaws. Closing on the steel gap between links, cutting the wrong link, or letting a matching link pass each cost one of three hearts.

3.Checkpoints and gold

Every third level is a checkpoint. Reach it once in any run, then spend gold to start a later run from there. Gold is earned for every link you cut and every level you clear. There are no purchases.

4.Saved progress

Best score, gold and checkpoints are saved on your device only. Deleting the App resets them.
